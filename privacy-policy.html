1. Introduction and developer

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application Kids Math Games (application ID app.dotinfiny.kidsmathgames) handles information. The developer is Muhammad Asher Ansari, reachable at asheransar@gmail.com.

The learning activities work offline. The app has no account registration, cloud progress synchronization, purchases or subscriptions. Advertising may use an internet connection when the privacy and configuration checks described below permit it.

2. Children’s privacy and age suitability

The activities are designed for children aged approximately 3–10 with parent or guardian supervision. The selected learning stage is a broad age range, not a date of birth. Grown-up Settings and the Parent Area use a multiplication question to discourage access by young children; this is not identity verification or verified parental consent.

The app does not ask a child to provide a name, email address, precise location, photograph, recording or contact list. It does not include chat, public profiles or social posting. Parents should supervise advertising links, sharing and email actions.

3. Information collected and processed

The app processes learning preferences and game state locally to generate suitable questions, restore an unfinished round and calculate progress. These records are not sent to the developer by the app. There is no developer-operated learning-data server in this version.

When permitted to run, Google’s advertising and privacy services process network and service-related information. If you voluntarily contact support, your email provider delivers the sender information and message you choose to send.

4. Local learning progress and preferences

Private app storage contains the selected age range, preferred difficulty, practice-level choices, sound and reduced-motion preferences, language value and onboarding status. It also holds questions and expected answers for an active round, selected answers, attempts, feedback, tracing strokes, round results, completion times, accuracy, stars and unlock progress. Legacy music and voice preference values may remain in older saved records even though those features are unavailable.

The app keeps a previous snapshot locally to recover an interrupted save. It does not attach these records, tracing strokes or the chosen learning stage to advertising, feedback or share payloads.

5. Google AdMob advertising

The Android app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Automatic advertising eligibility does not by itself grant permission: the app waits for a current privacy-information update, any required privacy form, and a positive SDK permission result. Release advertising also requires developer-reviewed configuration and valid advertising IDs. Development builds use Google’s official test units.

Google’s SDK can process an IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad or app interactions, diagnostic data and permitted device or app identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosures.

6. Child-directed advertising safeguards

The app marks ad requests as child-directed, requests a G maximum content rating, disables personalized advertising and includes the non-personalized-ad request signal. Android advertising-ID and AdServices advertising, attribution and topics permissions are removed. Publisher first-party ID support is disabled before ad requests proceed. These restrictions do not guarantee that Google processes no identifiers or no network data.

Banners are limited to approved browsing/progress screens. Active gameplay, tracing, answer feedback, legal pages and app dialogs do not display ads. Interstitial opportunities are restricted to explicit exits from completed results and remain subject to the existing frequency and release-review gates. External advertising destinations have their own terms and privacy practices.

7. Consent and regional privacy considerations

UMP checks whether privacy messages or choices are required for the configured app and region. The app marks the user as under the age of consent for this process. It does not assume a child can provide legally valid consent. Where applicable requirements cannot be satisfied, advertising must remain blocked; learning remains available.

The former Contextual Ads toggle is no longer used as a permission mechanism. When Google requires an advertising privacy-options entry point, it appears in grown-up Settings. A failed privacy check does not unlock ads, and parents can retry the check from Settings. Regional rights and obligations depend on applicable law and service configuration; this policy does not certify compliance with any particular law.

8. Third-party SDKs and services

Google Mobile Ads and UMP provide advertising and privacy-message functionality when permitted. Their own services may process information outside your country and retain it under their policies. Consult Google’s published policies and controls for details.

Android, Google Play, the device’s email application and applications selected through the share sheet operate under their respective providers’ policies. The app does not automatically send email or post shared content.

9. Internet connectivity and offline features

Learning games, local progress and the bundled Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions are available without a network connection. Advertising, privacy-information refreshes, external websites, email delivery and store links may require internet access. A connection or ad-load failure should not prevent learning.

The Android package declares network access/network-state permissions and SDK-related wake-lock, foreground-service and install-referrer capabilities. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or device-location permissions. Permissions alone do not establish that every SDK capability is used.

10. Data storage and retention

Learning records and recovery snapshots remain in this app’s private storage until overwritten, removed by Android storage controls, or uninstalled. There is no automatic expiry schedule for local round history. Ad-frequency limits and Google’s privacy-message state may also be stored on the device.

Android app backup and device-transfer exclusions are configured for the app’s data. Local progress has no account-based recovery service. Google controls retention of data it processes; this app does not promise a deletion schedule for Google’s systems. Support email remains in the email systems used by you and the developer as needed to address the request and any applicable obligations; no fixed email-retention period is promised here.

11. Data deletion

To remove local progress, preferences and recovery snapshots, use Android Settings → Apps → Kids Math Games → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device), or uninstall the app. These actions remove access to local progress; the developer cannot restore it from an account.

Clearing app data does not erase emails already sent, content shared through another app, or data held independently by Google or another service. Contact the relevant provider for those records. You can ask the developer about support messages at the email below.

12. Security

Learning data uses Android’s private app storage and the operating system’s protections. The legal viewer loads bundled content with JavaScript, WebView file/content access and network loading disabled; external links are handed to an appropriate external application.

No storage or communications system is completely secure. Protect access to your device and email account, install app/OS updates and avoid including sensitive child information in support requests. The app does not claim its local snapshots are separately encrypted by the app.

13. Parents’ rights and contact requests

Parents or guardians can review local progress in the Parent Area, change available preferences and delete device-local data through Android. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to information held about you or your child, such as access, correction, deletion or objection.

Send requests to asheransar@gmail.com. Describe the issue without attaching a child’s learning records or sensitive information unless needed and agreed. The developer may need reasonable verification before responding to a request but cannot retrieve learning records that exist only on your device. Requests concerning data held by Google or another independent service may need to be directed to that provider.

14. Feedback and sharing

Feedback / Contact Us opens an email composer addressed to asheransar@gmail.com with the subject “Kids Math Games - Feedback”. You decide whether to send it. The app does not attach learning records, a child profile, device identifiers or other personal data. Information you add yourself is handled by your email provider and the recipient.

Share App opens Android’s native share sheet with a fixed app description and the Google Play link for this application ID. The app does not include progress or personal information in that message. The destination app handles any content you choose to share.

15. External links

Links can open Google’s policies, the Play Store, an email composer or another external destination. The app’s legal viewer does not load those sites inside its document view. External destinations are governed by their own notices and may process information independently. A parent or guardian should review them.

16. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when app features, service configurations or legal requirements change. The updated document will carry a revised Last Updated date and be distributed with an app update. Material changes may also require additional notice or choices where applicable. Previously installed app versions keep their bundled copy until updated.
